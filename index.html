How I troubleshoot a PMax campaign

The order I work through when an advertiser says their Performance Max campaign isn't delivering.

1

Serving & eligibility

Policy status, budget limits, billing, learning period and conversion tracking health.

2

Bidding & targets

Is the tROAS or tCPA realistic for the data the campaign has? Too tight a target throttles delivery.

3

Assets & signals

Asset group strength, audience signals, feed quality and final URL settings.

4

Auction insights

Who you're competing with, impression share and what changed in the auction.
